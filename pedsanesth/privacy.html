Nasma AI privacy policy

Nightshift Games · Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short

Nasma AI has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of our own. The calculator runs entirely on your device. Information leaves your device only when you press one of the AI buttons, and then it goes to Google Gemini.

What stays on your device

  • Everything you enter in the calculator (age, weight, height and so on) while you use it.
  • Your favorites, theme, and your Gemini API key if you choose to remember it.

These are kept in your browser's storage. We never see them. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

What is sent to Google Gemini, and when

Only when you press an AI button (such as Case Plan, Assess Risks or Ask Question), the app sends Google Gemini:

  • the patient details entered in the calculator (such as age, weight, height, sex and fasting time) and values the app calculated from them;
  • the app's reference drug doses, so the answer matches the calculator;
  • the clinical notes or question you typed.
Do not enter patient identifiers. Never type names, record numbers, dates of birth, addresses or any other information that could identify a patient. The AI uses your own Gemini key under Google's Gemini API terms. On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products and people may review it, so it is not suitable for protected health information.

Hosting and links

Nasma AI is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may log basic technical data such as your IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement). Reference and support links (for example Ko-fi) open other websites with their own policies.

Clinical use

Nasma AI is decision support for anesthesia professionals. It does not replace clinical judgment. Always verify doses against your institution's references.
