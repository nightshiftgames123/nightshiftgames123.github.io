Privacy Policy

Gravity Flip (Android) · Nightshift Games · Last updated: 14 July 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Gravity Flip. In short: the game itself collects nothing about you, but it shows ads, and the advertising service does collect some data. The details are below.

Information the game collects

None. Gravity Flip has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no servers of our own. We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information.

Your high score, coin balance, unlocked shapes, and settings are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes them. We never see them.

Advertising

Gravity Flip displays ads supplied by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google. To show ads, AdMob may collect and process data such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • device information (model, operating system version, language, coarse location derived from IP address)
  • information about ads shown to you (for example, whether an ad was viewed or clicked)

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Google also describes how it uses data from apps that use its services here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can limit ad personalisation at any time on your device, under Settings → Google → Ads, where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID.

Children

Gravity Flip is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Permissions

The game requests two Android permissions:

  • Internet access — used solely to load ads.
  • Vibration — used solely for the buzz when you crash. It can be turned off in the game's settings.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct, or delete. For data held by Google in connection with advertising, please refer to Google's privacy policy linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
